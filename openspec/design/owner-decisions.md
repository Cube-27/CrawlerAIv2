| 68 | Price over currency certainty (2026-10-18) | "Price is most important, you can't just skip a price because you couldn't figure out the currency." A money value with a grounded amount is always published. Its currency comes from the best evidence, in order: stated with the price (ISO code or an unambiguous symbol); the same item's structured data; a page-level declaration (`og:price:currency`/`product:price:currency`, or a page-state member named `currency`/`currencyCode`/`activeCurrency` holding an ISO 4217 code, generic names only, consistent across the page); an ambiguous symbol narrowed to one ISO currency by the page's locale or region signals (`html lang` region, `hreflang`, Content-Language, ccTLD), with lower confidence and the evidence noted; otherwise the amount with currency `null` (unknown) and lower confidence.
